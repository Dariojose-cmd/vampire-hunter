# Product: Vampire Hunter

Vampire Hunter finds "vampire subscriptions": forgotten recurring charges that quietly
drain money from a bank account. The user uploads a bank statement (CSV), the app detects
recurring charges, and shows each one as a vampire in a haunted crypt. The user decides
what to do with each vampire: stake it (cancel), spare it (keep), or hang garlic
(set a reminder before the next charge).

## Core promise
"Your bank data never leaves your computer." All processing happens in the browser.
No backend, no accounts, no analytics, no network calls with transaction data.

## Audience
Regular people, not developers. Copy must be short, clear, and fun.
Bilingual: English and Spanish (UI language switch).

## Tone
Spooky and playful, never confusing. The horror theme must explain the function:
- vampire = recurring charge
- bite = one charge
- blood drained = money spent
- blood loss per year = annualized cost
- stake = cancel, garlic = reminder, spare = keep
